*** Genre

    - Rougelite robot automation mining game with crafting and tech tree.

*** Design Pillars and artistic Goal

    - Self-Exploratory mechanics / story in favour to tutorials and handholding.
    - Failure is good, reward for experimentation.
    - Runs should be more or less unique?

*** Setting and Story

    - Human negligence made AI take over and destroy plant. Now you go back in to dig up
      some resources for a colony.

*** Audio and Visuals

    - 2dArt dark low res pixel art (like https://kenney-assets.itch.io/pixel-platformer)
    - 8bit chiptune?

*** Gameloops and Mechanics

    - procedurally generated world with biomes, mutations
    - Core Loop: Drop on planet (with a loadout) -> Collect Resources / Fight Rouge Robots -> Evacuate
    - Meta Loop: Craft -> Upgrade / Research -> Improve Tools

    - Mining via Robots:
        - Craft them with specialized eqiupment and tools.
        - Program them via manual control, ASM, lua, DQN, NEAT.

    - Sensors, Model Settings, Procedures, input variables are all unlocks.

    - Discover lost Wikipedia sites that tell the story / history.

    - Limit runs by: enemies (death), battery charge (can be improved), inventory size / escape rocket inv size
